Work at JavaJam

Barista

Our baristas are the heart of JavaJam, greeting customers with a smile and crafting quality coffee and tea beverages. This position requires strong customer service skills and the ability to work well in a fast-paced environment. Hourly wage: $12.00.

  • Prepare and serve coffee, tea, and specialty drinks
  • Take customer orders and process payments
  • Maintain a clean and organized work area
  • Restock supplies and ingredients as needed

Kitchen Assistant

The kitchen assistant helps prepare light meals, snacks, and baked goods served at JavaJam. This role requires attention to detail and knowledge of basic food safety practices. Hourly wage: $11.50.

  • Prepare salads, sandwiches, and baked goods
  • Follow food safety and sanitation guidelines
  • Assist with inventory and food storage
  • Wash dishes and keep the kitchen clean

Event Coordinator

The event coordinator organizes music nights, poetry readings, and open mic events at JavaJam. This role requires strong communication skills and creativity. Hourly wage: $14.00.

  • Schedule musicians and performers
  • Promote events on social media and in-store signage
  • Set up and break down event equipment
  • Coordinate with staff to ensure smooth events
